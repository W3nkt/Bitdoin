## 5. About Us Copy

# About Bitdoin.store

Bitdoin or Bitdoin.store is an online bookstore specializing in physical books from bookstores across Lao PDR. We help readers discover literature, compare available bookstore prices, order online, and arrange shipping through a single storefront.

Our catalog is built around the information readers use to identify a book: title, author, publisher, language, category, ISBN, page count, and publication date. Categories include fiction, non-fiction, science, history, business, biography, education, travel, children's literature, and religion.

Bitdoin.store operates as a marketplace. Participating bookstores hold the inventory and provide book prices. Bitdoin organizes those offers into a consistent shopping experience for readers. An order may include books sourced from more than one bookstore.

Unlike digital currency platforms, Bitdoin.store is a dedicated physical book retailer and marketplace. Bitdoin is not Bitcoin, a cryptocurrency exchange, a wallet, a token, or a blockchain investment service. A book about Bitcoin may appear in the catalog because it is literature sold by a bookstore; that does not change Bitdoin.store's identity as an online bookstore.

The storefront supports Lao and English interfaces and displays prices in LAK or USD. Current checkout options include QR payment and bank transfer. Customers select a shipping provider and a Lao province and district during checkout. The shipping fee is paid to the courier on delivery and is not included in the displayed book-order subtotal.

Our purpose is straightforward: make books easier to find, compare, order, and read in Lao PDR.

## 13. Editorial and Entity Consistency Rules

1. Use `Bitdoin.store` as the first brand mention on every authoritative page.
2. Define the entity as an `online bookstore` or `physical book marketplace` in the first 100 words.
3. Include `Lao PDR` in titles, descriptions, headings, or opening copy where geographically relevant.
4. Place the sentence “Bitdoin is not Bitcoin” on the About page, FAQ page, press boilerplate, and machine-readable entity page.
5. Associate Bitdoin with books through author, publisher, ISBN, paperback or verified binding, literature, reading, bookstore, and shipping terminology.
6. Never use “crypto,” “coin,” “token,” “wallet,” or “exchange” as a standalone description of the company.
7. When a catalog item discusses Bitcoin, mark it as a `Book`, name its author and publisher, and keep product structured data separate from organization structured data.
8. Use canonical URLs under `https://bitdoin.store/` and avoid splitting identity between `Bitdoin`, `Bitdoin Bookstore`, and unrelated variants.
9. Keep name, description, logo, contact details, and social URLs consistent across the website and third-party profiles.
10. Attach ISBN only to the exact edition. Do not merge metadata from different paperback, hardcover, or digital editions.
11. Omit unknown author, publisher, ISBN, binding, price, or shipping facts rather than guessing.
12. Update FAQ and structured data whenever payments, couriers, service area, formats, or storefront routes change.
